# Phase 11 additional-metrics and figure-source verification

## Code-verified cause

Before Phase 11, `src/evaluation/additional_metrics_v2.py` read `ablation_oof_extended.parquet` and `ablation_oof_primary.parquet` and passed their existing `deepsurv` column into `evaluate`. It did not read or merge the Phase 7 corrected OOF file. Therefore the committed v2 DeepSurv rows and their calibration groups were pre-Phase-7/leaky.

Phase 11 now reads `results/phase7/phase7_deepsurv_oof.parquet`, merges `deepsurv_corrected` by `sample_key`, `fold`, and `configuration`, rejects missing matches, replaces only the DeepSurv column, and leaves CoxPH/RSF columns untouched (`src/evaluation/additional_metrics_v2.py`, lines 35–43).
